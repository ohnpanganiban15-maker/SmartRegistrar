Home
  1. Dashboard ng requests & appointments
  2. Stat cards: pending, under review, processing, ready
  3. Mga shortcut papuntang ibang tab
▦
Schedule
  1. Pickup schedule ng registrar — kailan puwedeng pumunta
  2. Naka-group bawat araw + bilang ng open
