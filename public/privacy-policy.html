Privacy Policy

CDLSHS Smart Registrar — Appointment & Document Request System

Last updated: October 2026 · Applies to the web app and the Android app.

This Privacy Policy explains what information the CDLSHS Smart Registrar collects, why it is collected, and how it is handled. By creating an account or signing in, you agree to this policy.

1. Information we collect

Information you provide directly when you register or complete your profile:

  • Identity — first name, middle name, last name
  • Academic — Learner Reference Number (LRN), grade/year level, and strand or track
  • Contact — email address and mobile number
  • Account — password (stored only as a one-way encrypted hash; it cannot be read back)
  • Service data — appointments booked, document requests submitted, messages, and uploaded reference files

When you sign in with Google, we receive your Google account ID, email address, and display name (and profile photo, if available) solely to verify your identity and create or link your account. We do not receive your Google password, and we do not request access to your Google Drive, Gmail, contacts, or any other Google data.

2. How we use your information

  • To create and secure your account and let you sign in
  • To process appointment bookings and document requests you submit
  • To send you status updates, one-time codes (OTP), and school announcements
  • To display registrar records and usage logs to authorized school staff
  • To keep the system secure, prevent misuse, and troubleshoot problems

3. Who can see your information

  • You — your own records and requests
  • Authorized CDLSHS registrar staff and administrators — as needed to perform their duties (verifying identity, approving requests, generating documents)

We do not sell, rent, trade, or share your personal information with advertisers or third-party marketers. Your data is not used for advertising.

4. How your data is stored and protected

  • Data is stored on a secured cloud database with restricted access
  • Passwords are protected using bcrypt, a one-way hashing algorithm
  • Sessions are protected using signed access tokens
  • Access to administrative functions is restricted by role and is recorded in activity logs

No internet-based system is completely secure, but we apply reasonable technical and organizational safeguards to protect your information.

5. Data retention

Records are retained for as long as your school account is active and as needed to fulfil registrar functions, comply with school policies, and meet legal requirements. Routine backups are kept for disaster recovery.

6. Your choices and rights

  • You may review and update your profile information at any time in My Profile
  • You may change your password from My Profile
  • You may request correction or deletion of your account data by contacting the registrar
  • You may stop using Google sign-in at any time by using email and password instead

7. Children's privacy

This system is intended for senior high school students of CDLSHS. Accounts are created for educational and administrative purposes under the supervision of the school. If you believe a student has provided information without proper consent, contact the registrar to have it removed.

8. Third-party services

  • Google Sign-In — used only to verify your identity. Governed by Google's own Privacy Policy and Terms of Service.
  • Cloud hosting and database providers — store the application data securely on our behalf.

9. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy as the system evolves. The "Last updated" date above will change whenever a revision is made. Continued use after changes constitutes acceptance of the updated policy.

10. Contact

For questions, requests, or concerns about your data, contact the CDLSHS Registrar Office through the app's chat feature, or by the official contact details of the school.
