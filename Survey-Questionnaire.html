Part I — Functionality

#Statement 54321
1.I can log in and access my account quickly and without errors.
2.I can submit a document request easily, and it is recorded correctly by the system.
3.The status of my request (Pending, Processing, Ready for Pickup, Completed) and the pickup date set by the registrar are accurate and always up to date.
4.I receive notifications/alerts about the updates on my requests.
5.I can change my own password easily, and the system confirms that it was updated successfully.

Part II — Design

#Statement 54321
1.The layout and overall appearance of the system are clean, modern, and pleasing to the eye.
2.Buttons, menus, and navigation are easy to find and understand.
3.Text, fonts, and colors are readable and consistent throughout the system, in both light and dark mode.
4.The system is easy to use on a mobile phone, and the screen adapts well to different devices (browser and installed app).
5.The welcome animation, school logo, and visual theme make the system look professional and appropriate for CDLSHS.

Part III — Safety

#Statement 54321
1.I feel that my personal information is kept secure inside the system.
2.My account is protected — wrong passwords are rejected, and repeated failed logins temporarily lock the account.
3.Only the registrar/admin can process requests, manage records, export data, and back up the database; students cannot access other people's data.
4.The system automatically signs me out after a period of inactivity, and asks for confirmation before cancelling, rejecting, or deleting anything.
5.The system handles errors safely (it shows clear warning messages) and does not lose my submitted data.
